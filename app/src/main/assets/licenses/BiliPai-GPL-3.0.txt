Adaptations in StackLife 0.10.12 (paths relative to app/src/main/java/io/github/nakanomk/stacklife):

appearance/WallpaperHost.kt (WallpaperHost, wallpaperBackground) ← design-system/src/main/java/com/android/purebilibili/core/ui/wallpaper/GlobalWallpaperBackdrop.kt: independently record the wallpaper, then replay it in screen coordinates. No video player, foreground capture, or Android-13-only effect in the fallback path.
appearance/WallpaperPolicy.kt and BackgroundPhoto.kt ← the same upstream wallpaper directory, WallpaperTransformPolicy.kt, WallpaperReadabilityPolicy.kt and WallpaperPaletteStore.kt: visible crop geometry, five local luminance bands and a 4.5:1 contrast target. Private still images only; increased contrast or reduced transparency uses a solid page surface.
appearance/BackgroundStore.kt, images/StaticPhoto.kt and appearance/WallpaperControls.kt ← app/src/main/java/com/android/purebilibili/feature/profile/WallpaperImageImport.kt: copy and validate while the picker permission is valid. StackLife normalizes the static image and manages its draft and committed owners locally; imported URIs are not persisted or sent to a model.
appearance/NavigationFields.kt and AppearanceEditor.kt ← app/src/main/java/com/android/purebilibili/feature/settings/screen/BottomBarSettingsScreen.kt: separate complete order from visibility, preserve a visible start destination, and support reordering. Persistence, undo on cancel, and navigation are StackLife's own.
today/DayWheel.kt (projectDayWheel) ← app/src/main/java/com/android/purebilibili/feature/settings/screen/ThemeTimeWheelPicker.kt: visible position drives alpha, scale and perspective. Applied during drawing to freely scrollable events, without snapping, selection haptics or a repeating list.
