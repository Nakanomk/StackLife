The nested scroll observer, direction test, can-move-away check and edge latch in appearance/GestureHaptics.kt are adapted from BiliPai. StackLife's vertical lists admit only user scroll deltas and the kinetic fling from that input. A drag-and-drop auto-scroll or notification alignment has no such session. The observer neither consumes deltas nor replaces AndroidX overscroll. Per-scene settings stay independent of the master switch. Instead of a new scene provider, thread-local action wrapper or pulses at intermediate dock slots, the accepted destination in the dock/queue/stack, a released root swipe, or the day/week selection owner asks StackLifeTheme's existing system-gated Compose delegate for one effect. A cancelled gesture and its return animation never request feedback.
