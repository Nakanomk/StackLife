StackLife exports a closed set of typed logical preference fields instead of arbitrary SharedPreferences. BackupDevice and BackupDeviceDao stage a target-Keystore encrypted generation, bind its active pointer and a replayable non-secret preference journal to the local Room commit, and retain same-origin credentials only when that was actually reviewed. Widget templates omit launcher identities; package install receipts and Android permission grants are not transferred. The crash recovery protocol and identity rules are StackLife-specific, not provided by either upstream backup implementation. Complete licensing information: Tasks-org-GPL-3.0.txt.
