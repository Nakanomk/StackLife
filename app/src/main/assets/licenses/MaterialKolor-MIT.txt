material-kolor-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/materialkolor/ktx/{ImageSampling,ImageBitmap}.kt:
BackgroundStore.wallpaperSeed uses samplePixels, Celebi (up to 128 colors) and Score, from
the existing dependency. StackLife samples a bounded private thumbnail or the actual bundled
wallpaper. A manual pick reports an empty filtered palette. Follow-wallpaper mode can use the
dominant neutral tone and follows replacement of the selected source; the user's saved seed
is the fallback for an absent image. Full Apache-2.0 license for the Material Color Utilities:
Backdrop-Miuix-Apache-2.0.txt.
