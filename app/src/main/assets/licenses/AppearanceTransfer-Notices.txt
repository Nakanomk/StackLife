StackLife modifications: archive/ArchiveCodec.kt is a bounded streaming envelope with a final completion manifest, versioned typed category contract, per-member size and SHA-256, and local/central directory cross-check. Only canonical ASCII paths, ordinary STORED ZIP files, the typed full appearance record and its exact global/per-page private-photo closure are accepted in an appearance package. Staging uses generated private filenames rather than user-provided paths. archive/AppearanceArchive.kt and appearance/BackgroundStore.kt lease immutable photos against collection, publish verified files atomically, resolve identity collisions and hand a typed replacement to Planner. An independent user decision is required for each new preview after a stale revision. appearance/AppearanceTransfer.kt adapts the two system document launchers and confirmation dialog to these transactions. No skins, executable content, plugins, WebDAV, credentials, clipboard transfer, donor preferences, or direct configuration-file restore are used.
