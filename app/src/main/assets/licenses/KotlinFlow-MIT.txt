KotlinFlow 1.0.1 (https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/io/github/joaonart/kotlinflow/1.0.1/)
StackLife uses KotlinFlow's node measurements, right/left Bézier path (utils/EdgeUtils.kt), and pan/zoom.
The editor and expanded proposal draw arrowheads from the tangent at their actual position on the path.
The local proposal overview adapts components/MiniMap.kt: fit both snapshots to one logical bounding
box and draw changed nodes and edges without viewport controls.
